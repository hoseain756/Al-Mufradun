Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.hussein.almufradun

Effective Date: May 29, 2026

At Al-Mufradun (المفردون), we are committed to providing a spiritual, peaceful, and entirely private experience. This Privacy Policy details how we handle user information in our mobile application.

Core Principle: We believe in absolute privacy. Al-Mufradun does not collect, track, upload, or sell your personal details, location, or usage data. Everything remains securely on your device.

1. Comprehensive Free Use & Data Policy

  • The application is completely free of charge, contains no third-party advertisements, and features no tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect, sell, lease, or share any personal, device, or usage data with any third parties under any circumstances.

2. Device Permissions and Usage

To deliver essential offline utility, the application requests the following standard permissions:

Location Access
Purpose: Used solely to calculate exact prayer times and determine the Qibla compass direction relative to your physical location.
Handling: Coordinates are calculated and processed entirely locally on your device. This information is never uploaded to any external server, database, or third-party service.
Notifications
Purpose: Required to schedule and display daily Azkar reminders and Athan notification alerts on your device.
Handling: All notification schedules and alarms are managed locally by the operating system using native background schedulers. No remote push servers are used.

3. Data Storage & Local Persistence

  • Your settings, dhikr counts, and favorite items are stored strictly in your device's local memory (via SharedPreferences and local SQLite databases).
  • All prayer time updates and Quran databases are bundled internally or calculated locally, operating completely offline.

4. Children's Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal data, we do not knowingly or unknowingly process children's data.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately once updated on this page.

Support Contact

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy or the app in general, please contact us at:

support@almufradun.app
